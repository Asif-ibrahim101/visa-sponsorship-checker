Privacy policy

Visa Sponsorship Checker (UK) · Last updated 13 November 2026

The short version

The extension does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any of your data. Everything it does happens inside your own browser.

What it reads

  • On pages you open, it checks whether the page is a job listing. If it is, it reads the job title, employer, location, salary and description so it can show a badge. If it isn't, it does nothing.
  • It never visits job sites in the background and never reads pages you haven't opened.

What it stores, and where

All of the following stays on your device, in your browser's own extension storage, and is never sent anywhere:

  • A copy of the public Home Office sponsor register.
  • Your settings (salary floor, extra phrases, display options).
  • Your corrections (for example "this company sponsors").
  • What it learnt from adverts you opened (sponsorship wording, salary, experience required), so badges stay correct when you scroll back.

Removing the extension deletes all of it.

Network requests

The only requests the extension makes are to GOV.UK (www.gov.uk and assets.publishing.service.gov.uk) to download the public Register of Licensed Sponsors (Workers), once a week or when you press "Refresh register". These requests contain no information about you or what you browse.

No tracking

There are no accounts, analytics, advertising, cookies or third-party services.
